# Decisions (sample)

## D-0001 - Go back and up, not forward (Day 2)

**Decided by:** the owner
**Decision:** New space goes in a rear wing and a second floor over the back of the house. The street face stays one storey.
**Why:** "I don't want it to look like a different house from the street."
**Instead of:** A full second floor over the whole house; a front addition.
**Affects:** brief, "Size and massing"; variant `a-rear-wing-up`.
