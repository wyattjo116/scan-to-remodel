# Credits

The interview method in this skill (the design tree, rounds over the frontier, a recommended answer with each question, "facts are the interviewer's job, decisions are the user's", and writing documents during the session) is adapted from two skills by Matt Pocock:

- `grilling` (invoked by `grill-me`): https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling
- `grill-with-docs`: https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/grill-with-docs

Those skills are for software plans and write a glossary and architecture decision records. This one applies the same method to a home-remodel brief and writes a brief, a glossary and a decision log. Parts of the method's wording follow the original closely.
