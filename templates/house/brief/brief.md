# The brief

The standing brief for this house. Every plan, model, render and estimate is judged against it. When a goal changes, change it here first, then record the change in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Each line is marked **Stated** (the owner said it, with the date), **Working** (inferred from the owner's choices; confirm before relying on it) or **Open** (not answered yet). Do not turn an Open line into a goal by guessing. Ask.

## Why and for whom

- **Open.** Who lives here and for how long.
- **Open.** What is wrong with the house today.
- **Open.** What must not be lost.

## Money

- **Open.** Hard ceiling.
- **Open.** What gives way first if the estimate runs over.
